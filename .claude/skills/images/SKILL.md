# Images

Check the images directory's CLAUDE.md for how this project stores and imports images.

- **Source** from free, no-attribution-required libraries: Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay. Check the individual image's license — "free" sites mix terms.
- **What to look for**: commercial use without credit; resolution to cover the largest display size (not 5× it); WebP/AVIF, or something worth converting; a subject that still works cropped.
- **Create instead of source** when the image must show this product (screenshots, diagrams) or match its exact style — generate or draw it rather than stretching a stock photo into the job.
